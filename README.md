>[!NOTE]
>## Custom trim materials (data packs)
>
>Trim **patterns** are limited to the 18 vanilla templates, but trim **materials** are data-driven.
>Any trim material added by a data pack works on shields automatically, with no extra setup.
>
>A trim's colours come from the material's palette texture (`palette_id`). That texture lives in a **resource pack**, not in the data pack.
>If the palette is missing (for example, a data pack installed without its companion resource pack), Shield Trims falls back to tinting the trim with the material's name colour (`description.color`).
>This fallback is only an approximation. It is darker and flatter than a hand-made palette, and bright colours cannot be fully reproduced.
>
>**I strongly recommend installing the official resource pack that comes with the data pack.**
>
>### Example: Colorful Trims
>
>[Colorful Trims](https://modrinth.com/datapack/colorful-trims) turns the 16 dyes into trim materials. Its colours are defined in a separate resource pack.
>

>To get the intended colours, use one of these:
>- the **data pack** in your world **and** the **resource pack** enabled on every client, or
>- the **mod version** of Colorful Trims, which bundles both. It must be installed on the server and on every client.
